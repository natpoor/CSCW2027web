To present a paper at the ACM CSCW conference, you must have a paper accepted at one of two journals:
- [PACM HCI/CSCW](https://dl.acm.org/journal/pacmhci/tracks/cscw)
- [ToCHI](https://dl.acm.org/journal/tochi/author-guidelines) (if the paper meets the CSCW Scope criteria on the link above)


{{< inline-heading >}}
## [Submissions to the Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction - CSCW are now open.](https://dl.acm.org/journal/pacmhci/tracks/cscw)

*There is no secret deadline!* If your paper is accepted before the program is set, you’ll be invited to present at CSCW 2027.
