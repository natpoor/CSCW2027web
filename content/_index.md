# THE 30TH ACM CONFERENCE ON COMPUTER-SUPPORTED COOPERATIVE WORK AND SOCIAL COMPUTING


---


{{< inline-heading >}}
## CSCW 2027 will be held in Philadelphia, PA, USA &mdash; October 16-20, 2027.

CSCW is the premier venue for research in the design and use of technologies that affect groups, organizations, communities, and networks. Bringing together top researchers and practitioners, CSCW explores the technical, social, material, and theoretical challenges of designing technology to support collaborative work and life activities.
{{< /inline-heading >}}

## [SUBMIT NOW](https://dl.acm.org/journal/pacmhci/tracks/cscw)
Submissions to the Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction - CSCW are now open. **There is no secret deadline!** If your paper is accepted before the program is set, you'll be invited to present at CSCW 2027.


Details on other ways to participate, registration, etc. will be posted soon. If you have any questions, concerns, or suggestions on how to make the conference great, please get in touch: chairs2027@cscw.acm.org
